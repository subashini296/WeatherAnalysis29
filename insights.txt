WEATHER PATTERN ANALYSIS - KEY INSIGHTS
=======================================
1. Dataset: 2192 daily records (2020-01-01 to 2025-12-31).
2. Hottest month: May (32.6 °C avg).
3. Wettest month: Oct (335 mm/yr avg); driest: Feb.
4. Wettest year: 2022 (1724 mm); driest year: 2020 (1418 mm).
5. Long-term temperature trend: -0.063 °C per year (cooling).
6. Rainfall-humidity correlation: 0.36 (higher humidity -> more rain).
7. Hot days (>= 38.2 °C): 110; heavy rain days (>= 50 mm): 12.
8. Random Forest rain-tomorrow model accuracy: 74.2%.
   Top predictors: humidity, month, temp_max.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Plan outdoor/agri activities outside the monsoon peaks.
